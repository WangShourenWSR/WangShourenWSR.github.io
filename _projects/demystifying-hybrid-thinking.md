[Demystifying Hybrid Thinking: Can LLMs Truly Switch Between Think and No-Think?](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.12680) examines whether current hybrid-thinking models genuinely separate their reasoning modes.

We show that reasoning behavior can leak into no-think outputs, identify key training factors behind this behavior, and develop a practical recipe that makes no-think responses shorter and more controllable while preserving performance.

The paper was accepted to the EMNLP 2026 Main Conference.
