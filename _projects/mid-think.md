[Mid-Think: Training-Free Intermediate-Budget Reasoning via Token-Level Triggers](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.299/) studies the token-level cues that induce or suppress reasoning.

The project introduces a training-free format for intermediate-budget reasoning, providing a controllable middle ground between full think and no-think behavior without additional model training.

The paper was published in Findings of ACL 2026.
