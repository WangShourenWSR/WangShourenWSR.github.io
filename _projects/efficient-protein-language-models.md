Protein language models have become important tools for variant-effect prediction, functional annotation, structure prediction, and protein engineering, but their increasing scale creates substantial computational and accessibility costs.

Our TMLR survey, [A Survey on Efficient Protein Language Models](https://openreview.net/forum?id=PTReuOwsXz), organizes the literature along four dimensions:

- dataset efficiency;
- architecture efficiency;
- training efficiency; and
- inference efficiency.

The accompanying [repository](https://github.com/SR-A-W/efficient-protein-language-model-survey) provides the paper, figures, versioned releases, and a maintained reading list.
